Call that level n. This ladder builds up to it from plain Python, pushes one rung past it into level n+1 (multi-class systems), and now one more rung into n+2 — mini in-memory databases, design patterns, testing, and concurrency. Every question has a solution you can reveal — but it'll ask what you're doing first.
